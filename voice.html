😶 Face & voice for the smoke cloud

Keep this page open in a browser on the PC while the cloud is on. It watches your face with your webcam and copies your expressions onto the cloud (it sends only numbers like "mouth half open", never video or audio). If it can't see you, the cloud shows a blank face.

Webcam already used by OBS? In OBS, click the ⚙ next to Start Virtual Camera (Controls dock), set Output Type to Source and pick your webcam source, press OK, then Start Virtual Camera. This page then uses OBS Virtual Camera by itself.
